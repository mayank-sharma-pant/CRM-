Perioxia CRM for field sales.

Sign in to your Perioxia company account and take your pipeline with you. This app is for existing Perioxia customers — your company creates the account on the web; the app is where your sales team works in the field.

What you can do:
- Work your leads: open a lead, update its stage, add notes.
- Complete follow-ups so nothing slips.
- View GST invoices, with CGST/SGST/IGST lines when your company is set up for GST.

Security:
- Two-factor authentication (TOTP) is supported when your company enables it.
- Your session token is held in the device's secure storage.

Privacy:
- The app handles your account email and the CRM records your company already gives you access to.
- No advertising SDK, no third-party analytics, no location tracking, no data sale.
- Privacy policy: https://crm.perioxia.com/privacy

This app does not create a company by itself as the primary path — start your trial on the web.
